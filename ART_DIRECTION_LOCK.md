# 忍道 - SHINOBI ROGUE Art Direction Lock (Ver.1.0.53)

Approved visual direction as of 2026-09-08. These are the canonical references for implementation; title and art can still be revised later without changing core rules.

## Core style
- Super-deformed/chibi Japanese ninja fantasy.
- Readable at iPhone size; bold silhouette before fine detail.
- Palette: charcoal/black, crimson red, warm beige/brown, muted gold/silver, deep purple for occult/dark-market elements.
- Main character: 2-head-tall black ninja, red scarf, silver forehead protector, lower face covered, sharp half-lidded brown eyes.
- Avoid direct copying of any existing franchise. Use room/corridor readability and Japanese dungeon atmosphere as broad genre inspiration only.

## Characters
- Protagonist: approved `ちび忍者キャラクターシート.png`.
- Ninja hounds: both cute tan early-game and dark aggressive stronger variant approved.
- Regular enemies: bandit/ninja/samurai/yokai families approved; runtime kinds map to these silhouettes.
- Traveler merchant: friendly green/tan traveling peddler with large pack.
- Dark merchant: hooded black/deep-purple dealer with talismans/rare goods.
- Village NPCs: blacksmith and ninjutsu researcher follow `忍道_キャラクター総合設定シート.png`.
- Bosses: armored samurai, ninja leader, oni heavy, shadow sorcerer; final-floor variant may combine shadow/leader motifs.

## Items and dungeon props
- Weapons/armor: ninja blade, wakizashi, kunai, shuriken, explosive shuriken; black ninja wear, forehead protector, gauntlets, tabi, red scarf.
- Scrolls: small/medium/large/ultimate differentiated by green/blue/red/purple-gold.
- Consumables: rice ball, large rice ball, medicine, antidote/energy bottles, smoke item, blinding tags.
- Dungeon props: stone floor/walls/stairs, chests, crates, jars, locked doors, lanterns, talismans, shop curtain, pit/spike/seal traps.

## Runtime implementation rule
Detailed concept art is not embedded in the Web build. Dungeon-scale entities use original compact vector tokens derived from the approved color/silhouette language so the game stays lightweight and readable. These tokens are replaceable with final sprite sheets later.
